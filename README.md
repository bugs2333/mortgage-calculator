# Mortgage Calculator

A simple and easy-to-use mortgage calculator for estimating monthly mortgage payments, total interest, and overall loan costs.

## About
<a href="https://www.openaccessjournals.com/blogs/mortgage-calculator">Mortgage Calculator</a> helps users understand the potential cost of a home loan by allowing them to enter key mortgage information and compare different loan scenarios.

Users can adjust the loan amount, interest rate, down payment, and repayment term to see how these factors may affect their estimated mortgage payments.

## Features

* Calculate estimated monthly mortgage payments
* Compare different interest rates
* Compare mortgage loan terms
* Estimate total interest
* Explore different down payment scenarios
* Compare mortgage scenarios
* Simple and user-friendly interface
* Works directly in a web browser

## How It Works

Enter the required mortgage information, including the loan amount, interest rate, and repayment term. The calculator processes the information and provides an estimated mortgage payment and related loan cost information.

Users can change the inputs to compare different mortgage scenarios.

## Disclaimer

The results provided by this calculator are estimates for informational and planning purposes only. Actual mortgage payments and borrowing costs may vary depending on the lender, loan terms, taxes, insurance, fees, and other expenses.

This calculator does not provide financial, mortgage, legal, or investment advice.
